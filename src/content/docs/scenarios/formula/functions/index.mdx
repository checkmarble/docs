When building a rule, you often need to apply some complex calculations on your operands or take historical data into account. This is why Marble offers a lot of **functions**, that you can use to build a rule or a trigger.

Here is the list of functions you can use when building a formula:

* [Aggregations](/scenarios/formula/functions/aggregations/)
* [String similarity](/scenarios/formula/functions/string-similarity/)
* [Datetime functions](/scenarios/formula/functions/datetime-functions/)
* [Multiple of a threshold](multiple-of-a-threshold)
* [Extract part of a timestamp](/scenarios/formula/functions/extract-a-part-from-a-timestamp/)
