Once you've reviewed a case, you must then make the decision wether to Approve or Decline the case. In each Cases that are on the status of "**Block & Review**", you will have a different button on each rules, that will mention "**Approve or Decline**". When clicking on that button, you will be prompted to make a decision and comment the reason of you decision.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-1.png" width="750px" bordered />

You may also click on the button by opening the alerts in the top right

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-2.png" width="750px" bordered />

By clicking on that button a pop up will appear, prompting you to Approve or Decline the transaction, and then click on validate.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-3.png" width="500px" bordered />

The alerts status will then change from "**Approve or decline**" to "**Approve**", or an "**X**" icon.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-4.png" width="500px" bordered />

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-5.png" width="500px" bordered />

## Blocking Review - Screening Case

Certain cases will be from a screening alert while having the status of "**Approve or decline**". In this case you will have multiple hits to review before being able to Approve of decline. If the hits to review have not been reviewed yet, the user will get a warning to before being able to approve.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-6.png" width="500px" bordered />

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-7.png" bordered />

A left panel with appear on the right side. Here you will have a list of all the potential matches that are potentially on the sanctions list. Each potential match will have a similarity percentage.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-8.png" bordered />

For each person you can un-collapse the details to have a comprehensive look at all the information.&#x20;

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-9.png" bordered />

Once you have reviewed, you may choose if the person is indeed a match or not, and save the decision.

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-10.png" bordered />

<Screenshot src="case-manager/blocking-review/blocking-review-11.png" width="500px" bordered />

Once done, you may then "**Approve or decline**" the case.
