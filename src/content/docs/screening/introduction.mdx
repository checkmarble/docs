Marble can be used to perform Customer and Payment Screening against global sanctions, watchlists, PEPs and averse media, for instance in a context of authorizing bank transfers.

A Screening can be configured as a special type of rule, part of a scenario. It is configured to force the outcome of a decision if there are any potential sanction hits to review.

With Customer and Payment Screening with Marble you get :

* **Real‑time Customer & Payments Screening** against global sanctions, watchlists, PEPs and adverse media via [Opensanctions](https://www.opensanctions.org/) or [LexisNexis WorldCompliance data](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/global/en/products/worldcompliance-data)
* **AI Smart‑matching and noise filtering** to remove the clutter and reduce false positives by up to 80% 
* **Ultra flexible and granular rule builder** to configure screening programs tailored to your business reality and control the costs

It is a premium offering of Marble - get in touch with us to get a license key with access to sanction checking.
